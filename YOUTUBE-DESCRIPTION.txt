--- DESCRIPTION ---

An org chart should answer two questions in a second: who reports to whom, and how big each team is. With a large organisation, most charts either shrink to text nobody can read or need a column for every level.

Org Chart by TCViz is free — every feature, no licence, no limit on people, no watermark. Two columns draw it: the employee ID and the manager ID. Add names, job titles, departments and photos, and it opens as many levels as fit on screen with every name still readable.

WHAT YOU SEE IN THIS VIDEO
• Two fields, any depth: Employee ID and Manager ID
• Photos, job titles, departments and team size on every card
• Style templates and department colours
• Show only one team, with the breadcrumb to go back up
• Search: the chart opens the branch and finds the person
• Opening and closing one level at a time, the minimap and zoom
• Dotted-line managers for matrix teams
• Clicking a manager filters the whole report by their team
• Formatting directly on the canvas

WHAT IT DOES
• Employee and manager IDs, any number of levels, several tops
• Vacancies from rows without a name; dotted-line reporting as a second set of links
• Team size (direct reports and whole team) and a measure added up for each manager
• Compact layout so wide teams fit; top to bottom or left to right
• Colour by department, level or value; tinted cards; department groups
• Reporting loops, repeated IDs and missing managers are handled and reported
• Bookmarks, report page tooltips, conditional formatting, keyboard navigation, high contrast
• Format pane in 12 languages
• No network access: photos stay in your model, nothing leaves your report
