Quick Start

1. Add the visual to your report

In Power BI Desktop, go to the Visualizations pane → click the three dots → Get more visuals → search for Org Chart by TCViz.

2. Two fields draw the chart

Drag the person's ID to Employee ID and their manager's ID to Manager ID. The person with an empty Manager ID is the top of the chart. That is all the visual needs: it works out the levels itself, however deep the organisation is.

3. Make the cards useful

Add Name, Job title, Department and Photo. Put a measure in Value (cost, FTE, sales…) to show it on each card and add it up for each manager's team.

4. Pick a look

Format → Style template: Clean, Corporate, Colorful, Minimal or Compact. Anything you set by hand wins over the template.

Navigating a large organisation

  • Open levels to fit (on by default): the chart opens as many levels as fit with names still readable on screen, and folds the rest. Each folded card shows +N, the number of direct reports under it.
  • Collapse and expand any branch with the button under the card, or double-click the card. The toolbar opens or closes one level at a time across the whole chart, or everything at once with Expand all and Collapse all.
  • Show only this team: the button in the corner of a manager's card shows just their branch, with the path above — Everyone › CEO › CFO — to go back up one click at a time (breadcrumb).
  • Search by name, job title, department or ID: the chart opens the branch, centres the person and marks the card. Press Enter for the next match.
  • Zoom with the toolbar or Ctrl + mouse wheel, pan by dragging the background or with the mouse wheel, Fit to see everything, and the minimap in the corner to jump anywhere.
  • The branches you open and the team you focus on are remembered when filters change and when the data refreshes.

Toolbar

Format on the canvas

In format mode, click a card's text to change font, size and colour, the card itself for fill and border, or a line for its colour and shape — without opening the Format pane.

Selection & filtering

  • Click a card to filter the page by that person — or, for a manager, by their whole team. Click again to clear, Ctrl+click to add more, click the background to clear.
  • Selections in other visuals highlight the cards they touch (with a Value measure) and dim the rest.
  • Bookmarks restore the selection: the chart opens the branches of the selected people and centres them.
  • Right-click a card for the Power BI context menu (Drill through, Include, Exclude…).

Accessibility

  • Keyboard: Tab into the chart, then move through the tree — top to bottom: ↑ manager, ↓ first report (opens the branch if needed), ← → colleagues; left to right the arrows turn with the chart. Home goes to the top, Enter or Space selects (Ctrl to add), +/- expand and collapse, F shows only that team, Esc clears, Shift+F10 opens the context menu.
  • Screen readers: the chart is a tree; each card is announced with name, job title, department, number of direct reports, level and whether it is expanded.
  • High contrast: with a Windows high-contrast theme, cards, lines and text use the system colours.
  • 4 colour-blind safe palettes (Okabe-Ito and Paul Tol).

Real data: what the chart does with it

  • Several people without a manager: several charts side by side.
  • A Manager ID that is not an employee: that person's team is shown at the top, and the warning icon lists the missing IDs.
  • A reporting loop (A reports to B, B reports to A): the loop is broken, one of them becomes a top, and the warning icon says where.
  • A repeated Employee ID: the first row is used, and the warning icon lists it.
  • Someone who is their own manager: treated as a top.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature on this page is included, with no licence, no limit on the number of people, no trial and no watermark.

Is it only for people?

No. Any parent–child table works: a chart of accounts, cost centres, legal entities, a product tree. Put the item's ID in Employee ID and its parent's ID in Manager ID.

How deep can the organisation be?

There is no level limit: the levels come from the Manager ID chain, not from separate columns.

How do I show a vacancy?

Add a row for the position with an Employee ID, a Manager ID and an empty Name. It is drawn as Vacant with a dashed border.

Someone reports to two managers. Can I show both?

Yes: the main manager in Manager ID and the second one in Dotted-line manager ID. The second link is drawn as a dashed curved line, and the tooltip names it.

How do I get photos into the model?

As Base64 text: a column whose values start with data:image/jpeg;base64,. Keep them small — Power BI cuts text values longer than 32,766 characters; a 96×96 JPEG is about 5,000. Set the column's data category to Image URL if you also want to see the photos in a table.

Why are web links to photos not loaded?

The visual has no network permission, so it cannot load anything from outside your report — and cannot send anything either. See Privacy.

What do bookmarks remember?

Power BI bookmarks keep a custom visual's selection: applying one selects the same people again, opens their branches and centres them. The branches you open by hand and the team you focus on are kept while you work — through filters and refreshes — but a bookmark does not store them; that is how bookmarks work for all custom visuals.

Clicking a manager filters everyone under them. Can it filter just the manager?

Turn off Layout → Click selects the whole team.

Can I drill down?

The org chart is already the hierarchy: open a branch, or use Show only this team to go into it, and the breadcrumb to come back up. Power BI's drill buttons apply to hierarchies of columns, which an employee–manager table does not have.

Which languages is the Format pane in?

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Dutch, Polish, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Korean. It follows the language of Power BI.

Does my data leave Power BI?

No. The visual has no network permission: it cannot send or load anything. See Privacy.
