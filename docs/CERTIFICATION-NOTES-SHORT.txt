HOW TO TEST (sample .pbix)
1. Employee ID + Manager ID: chart draws; a manager card shows the team total;
   tooltip shows own value and the team value.
2. Make Value blank for one manager: his row is dropped, his team goes to the top,
   the warning icon names the ID and the fix. Add a row count to Tooltips: he returns.
3. Format > Style template: switch the 6 styles and Report theme.
4. Format > Department colors: change one department; it wins over the style.
5. Format > Accessibility: High contrast On (black on white); Minimum text size 14
   (cards grow); Tab to a card to see the focus color/width.
6. Click a manager: page filters by the team. Bookmarks restore the selection.
