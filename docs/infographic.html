Avoid common mistakes

  • IDs as text in one column, numbers in the other
  • Photos as web links
  • Huge photos (over 32,766 characters)
  • 96×96 JPEG ≈ 5,000 characters
  • Watch the warning icon: loops, repeats, missing managers
