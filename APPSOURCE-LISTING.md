## Description

```
An org chart should answer two questions in a second: who reports to whom, and how big each team is. With a large organisation, most charts either shrink to text nobody can read or need a separate column for every level.

Org Chart by TCViz is free — every feature, no licence, no limit on people, no watermark. Two columns draw it: the employee ID and the manager ID, at any depth. Add names, job titles, departments and photos, and it opens as many levels as fit on screen with every name still readable.

FIND ANYONE IN A LARGE ORGANISATION

• Opens as many levels as fit with readable names; each folded card shows how many people report to it
• Open or close one level at a time, or one branch, or everything
• Show only one team, with a breadcrumb (Everyone › CEO › CFO) to go back up
• Search by name, job title, department or ID: the branch opens and the person is marked
• Zoom, pan, fit to view and a minimap
• Compact layout: people without reports are listed under their manager, so wide teams fit

THE CARDS

• Photo or initials, name, job title, department
• Team size: direct reports, whole team, or both
• A measure on each card (cost, FTE, sales) and added up for each manager's team
• Vacancies from rows without a name
• Dotted-line managers for matrix teams, drawn as a second dashed link

LOOK

• Style templates: Clean, Corporate, Colorful, Minimal, Compact
• Colour by department, by level, by a value, or a single colour; tinted cards and photo rings
• Department groups, elbow, straight or curved lines, labels with team size or value
• Conditional formatting (fx) on card colour; top to bottom or left to right

WORKS LIKE A NATIVE VISUAL

• Click a manager to filter the page by their whole team, or just by them; Ctrl+click for more
• Highlighting from other visuals, bookmarks that restore the selection, context menu
• Tooltips and report page tooltips; number formats from your model
• Format on the canvas: click the text, a card or a line to format it
• Keyboard navigation through the tree with screen reader labels, high contrast
• Format pane in 12 languages
• More than 30,000 rows in the Power BI service: the visual asks for the rest

REAL DATA

Several people at the top, managers that are not in the list, reporting loops and repeated IDs do not break the chart: it draws what it can and the warning icon says what it found.

PRIVACY

The visual has no network permission, so Power BI does not let it send or load anything. That is why photos have to be stored in the model as Base64: a link to a photo on a web server is not loaded, on purpose. Names and photos never leave your report.

GETTING STARTED

1. Drag the person's ID to Employee ID and their manager's ID to Manager ID.
2. Optional: Name, Job title, Department, Photo, and a measure in Value.
3. Format → Style template.

Documentation, FAQ and examples: https://tinocallarisa-web.github.io/org-chart/support.html
Questions: https://github.com/tinocallarisa-web/org-chart/discussions
```
